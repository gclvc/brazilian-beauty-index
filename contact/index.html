1. Editorial and story tips

Pitches, salon reports, tip-offs and contributor enquiries go here. We read every message, even when we cannot run the story.

Editorial inbox
editorial@brazilianbeautyindex.com

Reply within 5 working days.

What tends to land:

  • Brazilian beauty industry stories — regulatory shifts at ANVISA, ownership changes at a major brand, factory or supply-chain reporting.
  • New product launches in the UK — brands arriving for the first time, with samples available for hands-on testing and a UK-based brand contact who can answer questions on record.
  • Salon professional reports — working stylists, colourists or trichologists with first-hand observations from the chair. Photos, before/after, and named clients (with consent) carry the pitch.
  • Investigations — safety, mislabelling, ASA breaches, formaldehyde levels in smoothing products. Send what you have and we will tell you whether we can take it further.

Less likely to run: generic round-ups, AI-generated copy, press releases without a UK angle, affiliate-only pitches, anything paywalled behind a follow-up call.

2. Corrections

If we have got a fact wrong, tell us. We treat corrections as a priority queue and reply faster than any other inbox.

Corrections inbox
correction@brazilianbeautyindex.com

Reply within 2 working days.

Please include the article URL, the exact passage, what is wrong, and where possible a source we can check. The full process — minor fixes vs. material corrections vs. retractions — is set out in the Correction Policy.

3. Privacy and GDPR requests

Use this address for data subject access requests (DSARs), erasure requests, marketing objections, and any other right you want to exercise under the UK GDPR or the Data Protection Act 2018.

Privacy / DPO inbox
privacy@brazilianbeautyindex.com

Full reply within 30 days, as required by Article 12(3) of the UK GDPR. Most requests are closed faster.

If you are sending a DSAR, please confirm your identity (an email from the address tied to your account is usually enough) and tell us roughly when and how you interacted with the site so we can find the right records. The legal detail — lawful basis, retention, recipients, your rights, complaints to the ICO — sits in the Privacy Policy.
