Why We Write

Why This Site Exists

When we started distributing Brazilian hair care in the UK, there was almost nothing written about these brands in English. Salon owners had to rely on Portuguese-language content they couldn't read, or generic beauty blogs that had never touched the products.

Brazilian beauty — particularly professional keratin treatments — deserves proper coverage. We know these products better than anyone in Europe. So we write about them.

Every article is written from direct experience. When we say a treatment lasts 3–5 months, it's because we've tracked that across hundreds of salon applications. When we compare two products, it's because we've distributed both.
