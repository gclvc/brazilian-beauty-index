1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit. Cookies let the site remember things about your visit — your language preference, whether you have already dismissed a banner, an anonymous identifier for analytics. Similar technologies (local storage, session storage, pixel tags) work in much the same way and are covered by this policy.

2. Why we use them

We use cookies for three reasons:

  • to make the site work (e.g. remembering your cookie choice itself);
  • to understand which articles and tools are useful, in aggregate, so we know what to write more of;
  • to remember small functional preferences if you opt in to them (for example, hair-type segment for newsletter personalisation).

We do not use cookies for cross-site behavioural advertising, profiling for third parties, or anything that requires a tracking pixel from an ad network.

5. How to manage your cookies

On this site

You can change or withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clicking the button below — this will clear your previous choice and ask again on your next page view.

In your browser

All major browsers let you see, block or delete cookies. Where you find the controls depends on the browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari (macOS)
  • Apple Safari (iOS)
  • Microsoft Edge

Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the site from working — for example, forms that rely on a session token.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The "Do Not Track" header has been deprecated and we do not act on it. We do respect the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal: if your browser sends GPC, we treat it as a request to opt out of non-essential cookies and skip setting analytics and functional cookies for that session.

6. Changes to this policy

If we add a cookie, remove one, or change a supplier, this page is updated and the "Last updated" date at the top changes. Where a change introduces a new category of cookie, the banner will re-prompt for consent.
